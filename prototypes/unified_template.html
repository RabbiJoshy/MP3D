Speech evidence

A map made from words

MP3D maps patterns in MPs’ expressed positions and speech topics. Proximity is a compressed view of those patterns, not a guarantee of agreement; select an MP to compare the evidence behind nearby neighbours. Choose a topic to follow the divide through the centre of the sphere.

All Commons shows the shared political space. Party Removed subtracts each MP’s party average to reveal differences within parties.

Twenty belief scales use real speech excerpts. Other topics show what MPs talk about. In the topic chart, position runs horizontally and attention to the issue runs vertically. Arrows connect earlier and recent positions where evidence is available.

Your star combines the answers you give, with extra weight for your priorities. MP matches require at least three shared directional answers and are ranked by recorded agreement; map distance only breaks ties. The star is a provisional projection, not a validated estimate of your political identity. Parties are ordered by distance to their average. These are exploratory comparisons, not voting recommendations or percentages of agreement. Neutral answers centre your star; skipped questions do not count.

Speeches only capture part of a person’s views. Missing evidence is not disagreement. Your answers stay in this page and reset when you reload.
