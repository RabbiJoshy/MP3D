Education: who wants what

Work in progress — developmental method, Education only

An AI model reads Commons speeches on education and extracts the specific change each MP argues for or against. A second, stricter check then keeps only claims where the speech clearly takes that side; everything else is set aside, and is listed so you can see it.

  • Developmental: the measure groups and the checks are experimental and will change. There is deliberately no map or axis yet; too few measures have two MPs on each side.
  • Checked against 38 hand-read claims: the strict check agreed with the human-style reading on those (see the badges). The rest are not hand-checked.
  • One topic only (Education, this Parliament). Counts are MPs with at least one accepted speech, not votes. Speech links go to TheyWorkForYou.
