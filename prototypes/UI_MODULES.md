# MP3D modes — 10 November 2026

- Explore: full canvas, initially All topics (no active topic effects); topic selection enables its axis. All topics clears axis, comparison and topic pull. Topic-specific evidence is omitted when no topic is selected.
- Find my place: nonmodal dialog shown with `show()`, floating left on desktop and below the map on mobile. The canvas never leaves dualWrap. Question alignment is horizontal on entry; a dashed, translated A/B guide passes through the visitor marker. The guide indicates the question direction, not a calibrated displacement prediction. Existing provisional placement and three-shared-answer match safeguards remain.
- Topic relationships: separate full-page surface with a flat link diagram, ranked neighbours, and a divisive × distinguishing scatter. Derived on demand from the active speech-period scores; no new data pipeline. Twenty scored topics only. Links use absolute observed Pearson correlation with at least 30 common MPs. Divisiveness = 4p(1−p) excluding neutral scores; distinguishing = mean absolute eligible correlation. Ring position has no metric meaning. Associations can reflect party and coverage and are not validated predictive information. Method detail is tucked into a disclosure.

Map changes: no directional red/green hemisphere glow; neutral axis/pole labels in opaque backgrounds beyond the sphere edge; shared zoom state constrained to 55–250%, with wheel, pinch, +/- and reset. Pinch implemented but not hardware-tested. Fullscreen and existing settings retained. Quiz switches dual layout to the full map to avoid shrinking the shared canvas.

Validation: existing visitor and selection checks; synthetic relationship tests for sign reversal, minimum overlap, missing and constant scores. Browser tested navigation, selected-topic handoff, All topics, 2D/3D zoom buttons, desktop/mobile quiz, original canvas parent, nonmodal dialog semantics, source disclosures and absence of console errors. No score or underlying MP-coordinate changes.
