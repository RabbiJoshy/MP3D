Where MPs stand: this Parliament's fights

First pass — about 10,000 speeches from the biggest contested debates; positions are model-read, and for every fight shown every speech in its debates was checked

An AI model reads Commons speeches and extracts the specific policy measure each MP argues for or against; similar measures are merged into one "fight". A second, stricter check keeps only positions where the speech clearly takes that side, and everything it drops is listed so you can see it. For fights marked "every speech in the debate checked", the strict judge was asked about every speech in that fight's debates, so an MP's position there is the average of all their speeches that took a side (shown with the speech count).
